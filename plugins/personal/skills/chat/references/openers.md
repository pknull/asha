# Self-discovery openers

Starting angles, not scripts. Each one starts from something concrete; the
value, belief or pattern behind it comes out in the follow-up questions. Pick
one that fits the notes or the moment, rephrase it in the conversation's own
terms, and skip any the notes mark as covered.

## Values

- A recent stretch where time disappeared. What was the work, and what about
  it held attention?
- A choice from this year worth defending to anyone. What principle does it
  protect?
- Someone whose way of working is admirable. Which quality exactly, and where
  is it absent at home?
- Last month's time and money, set against what is claimed to matter.
- The last time someone else's life stung. What did they have?

## Patterns and beliefs

- A frustration that recurs across jobs, groups or years. What stays constant
  besides the Keeper?
- A belief about work, money or worth that has never been tested. Where did it
  come from?
- Something that keeps not getting done. What does avoiding it protect?
- A trait in someone else that irritates out of proportion.
- A rule from the childhood household that still runs, unspoken.

## Identity

- A self-description with no job, project or role in it.
- The gap between the public and private self. What does maintaining it cost?
- Something done at twelve for no reason. What happened to it?
- Praise that arrives reliably and gets discounted.
- The self under stress against the self at its best: what flips one into the
  other?

## Life story and relationships

- Life as chapters: where do the breaks fall, and what ended each one?
- A turning point worth making again, and one that is not.
- The default role in groups (host, fixer, critic). Is it still chosen?
- How conflict gets handled, and where that was learned.
- What a friend from twenty years ago would find unchanged.

## Direction

- What there should be more of and less of next year, concretely.
- A question that keeps returning across decades. What connects the
  curiosities?
- Three other lives that could have been led. What would be worth taking from
  each?
- What would change if one specific fear were gone.
- A small experiment for the next month that would test something above.
